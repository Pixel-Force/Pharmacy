Pharmaceutical Law & Governance

Pharmacy Dispensing & Compliance Policy

Legal frameworks, prescription verification requirements, controlled substances restrictions, and patient privacy standards.

Operating License & Regulatory College Registration

CuraRx Pharmacy operates under state dispensary permit #RPH-942817-A, governed by the Board of Pharmacy and compliant with federal FDA (Food and Drug Administration) regulations and WHO Good Pharmacy Practice standards.

1. Mandatory Prescription Verification Protocol

In strict compliance with international pharmaceutical statutes, no medication classified as a Prescription-Only Medicine (Rx / POM) will be dispensed, fulfilled, or shipped without prior verification by a licensed clinical pharmacist.

  • Valid Prescriber: Prescriptions must be authored by a licensed physician, dentist, nurse practitioner, or veterinarian authorized to prescribe in the patient's jurisdiction.
  • Prescription Legibility & Integrity: Prescriptions submitted via photo or scan must clearly depict the patient's full legal name, date of birth, prescriber's clinic name and license number, date of issue, medication name, strength, dosage instructions, quantity, and authorized refills.
  • 15-Minute Pharmacist Review: Every submitted prescription is evaluated by a Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) practitioner who conducts an algorithmic drug-drug interaction screen, duplicate therapy check, and allergy cross-reference against the patient's health profile.

2. Controlled Substances (Schedule II Narcotics) Prohibition

STRICT POLICY ON CONTROLLED NARCOTICS:
CuraRx does NOT sell, dispense, or mail-order Schedule II controlled substances (e.g. Oxycodone, Hydrocodone, Fentanyl, Morphine, Amphetamines/Adderall, Methylphenidate/Ritalin). Requests for Schedule II medications will be immediately declined and directed to physical clinical facilities.

3. Over-the-Counter (OTC) & Pseudoephedrine Regulations

Over-the-counter medications are sold in accordance with consumer safety laws and age requirements.

  • Pseudoephedrine Limits: Pursuant to Combat Methamphetamine Epidemic legislation, sales of nasal decongestants containing pseudoephedrine are restricted to a maximum of 3.6 grams per transaction and 9.0 grams per 30-day period.
  • Age Verification: Purchasers of cough syrups containing dextromethorphan or NSAID analgesics must be at least 18 years of age.

4. Cold-Chain Transportation & Biological Integrity

Temperature-sensitive pharmaceuticals (including insulins, GLP-1 receptor agonists, vaccines, and eye drops) require continuous thermal protection.

  • Temperature Monitored: Maintained strictly between 2°C and 8°C (36°F to 46°F) during all transit stages using calibrated digital dataloggers.
  • Insulated Containers: Shipped in certified pharmaceutical insulated coolers with phase-change cold packs guaranteeing up to 48 hours of thermal stability.

5. Patient Health Data Privacy (HIPAA & GDPR)

Confidentiality Commitment:
All patient health information (PHI), uploaded prescription images, clinical records, and consultation notes are encrypted at rest using AES-256 bit encryption and in transit using TLS 1.3. We strictly adhere to HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) and GDPR regulations. We never sell, lease, or monetize patient medical records.

6. Returns, Cancellations & Environmentally Safe Disposal

For public health and safety reasons, once a prescription medication has left our pharmacy dispensing facility, it cannot be returned or restocked under pharmaceutical law (to prevent tampering, contamination, or thermal degradation).

  • Damaged or Erroneous Dispensation: If you receive an incorrect product or a package with damaged tamper seals, notify our pharmacy within 24 hours for a full replacement and courier pickup.
  • Medicine Take-Back Program: Unused or expired medications should never be flushed down toilets. CuraRx provides free prepaid safe disposal mail-back envelopes upon request to protect community water supplies.
