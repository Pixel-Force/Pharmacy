DRAP Compliance & Quality Protocols

How CuraRx adheres to national pharmaceutical laws and patient safety mandates across Pakistan.

Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP) Certification

All therapeutic products, medical devices, and nutritional supplements cataloged and dispensed through CuraRx Pakistan are registered and approved by the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP) under the DRAP Act 2012 and Drugs Act 1976.

1. Sourcing Exclusively From Authorized Principal Depots

CuraRx operates a closed, zero-counterfeit distribution pipeline. Every stock batch is procured directly from the manufacturer's central depots (e.g. Getz Pharma, GSK Pakistan, Abbott Laboratories, Martin Dow, and The Searle Company) with invoice validation, batch certificate of analysis, and manufacturer seal verification.

2. Cold-Chain Storage & Transport Protocol

In Pakistan's severe summer climate, temperature fluctuations above 25°C can rapidly degrade insulin, vaccines, inhalers, and eye drops. CuraRx operates temperature-monitored warehouse chillers calibrated between 2°C and 8°C. During dispatch, orders are placed in insulated thermal pouches packed with food-grade gel ice packs, ensuring full medication potency upon delivery.

3. Licensed Pharmacists Supervision

In compliance with the Pharmacy Act 1967, every dispensing facility is supervised in-person by registered Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) practitioners holding Category 'A' licenses issued by the Pharmacy Council of Pakistan. Pharmacists perform mandatory pre-dispatch safety reviews for dosage and drug-drug interactions.

4. Consumer Rights & Price Transparency

All pharmaceutical medicines are sold in strict compliance with the Maximum Retail Price (MRP) mandated by the Federal Government of Pakistan and DRAP. Customers receive itemized official fiscal receipts with batch numbers and expiry dates printed clearly on the invoice.
